ERA 01 // PRE-APOCALYPSE

Mino Soft & The Sole Reader

Prior to the collapse of reality, Kim Dokja was a 28-year-old contractor in the Quality Assurance department of game development firm Mino Soft. Having survived a harrowing childhood and military service as a cook, his sole sanctuary was a web novel:

  • Novel: “Three Ways to Survive in a Ruined World” (TWSA / 멸살법)
  • Author: The enigmatic tls123
  • Duration: Read religiously every single day for 13 years (3,149 chapters)
  • Readership: By chapter 3,149, Dokja was the only reader remaining on the entire platform.
“On the subway commute home on Line 3, chapter 3,149 was published with a note: [Paid service will now commence].”
ERA 02 // POST-APOCALYPSE

The Star Stream Ascendance

When Dokkaebi Bihyung materialized in Seoul Subway Car 3707, Dokja used his exhaustive encyclopedic memory of TWSA to subvert the scenarios, protect his companions, and dismantle the tyrannies of the Bureau:

  • Sponsor Selection: Defiantly rejected all sponsor Constellations to walk his own independent path.
  • The 73rd Demon Realm: Sacrificed his own existence to spare Yoo Joonghyuk and ascended with the modifier “Demon King of Salvation”.
  • Nebula Founding: Founded <Kim Dokja's Company> to liberate incarnations across Earth and the Demon Realm.
  • Cosmic Apotheosis: Ascended as the Watcher of Light and Darkness, and ultimately discovered his true nature as The Most Ancient Dream.
