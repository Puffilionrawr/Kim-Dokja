# 🌌 Kim Dokja (김독자) | Demon King of Salvation — Character Portfolio

> *“I am Kim Dokja. An ordinary reader who reached the end of the world.”*  
> *“Tell me, Yoo Joonghyuk. If I don't give up on this story... will this story also not give up on me?”*

A modern, interactive, lore-accurate character portfolio website and Star Stream HUD interface for **Kim Dokja (김독자)**, the central protagonist of the masterwork Korean web novel and webtoon **Omniscient Reader’s Viewpoint (ORV / 전지적 독자 시점)** by **sing N song (싱숑)**.

---

## 🚀 Key Features & Novel Lore Accuracy

### 1. 🛡️ Star Stream HUD & Sci-Fi Webnovel Terminal
- **Aesthetic Design:** Deep space void (`#0b0e14`, `#121824`), Star Stream electric cyan (`#00e5ff`), constellation gold (`#f59e0b`), and clean white contrast matching Kim Dokja's iconic **Infinite Dimension Space Coat**.
- **Live Broadcast Ticker:** Real-time floating ticker displaying dynamic constellation reactions and coin sponsorships from Uriel, the Abyssal Black Flame Dragon, the Great Sage Heaven's Equal, and the Secretive Plotter.
- **Audio Synthesizer (Web Audio API):** Zero-dependency Star Stream dokkaebi system chimes and coin donation sound effects.

### 2. 📖 Biography & Origin Story
- **Pre-Apocalypse:** 28 years old, QA contract employee at *Mino Soft*, former military cook, and the solitary reader of the 3,149-chapter web novel *“Three Ways to Survive in a Ruined World”* (TWSA) by *tls123* across 13 years.
- **Post-Apocalypse:** Rejecting sponsor Constellations, founding **&lt;Kim Dokja's Company&gt;**, self-sacrifice in the 73rd Demon Realm, and ascending as the *Demon King of Salvation*, *Watcher of Light and Darkness*, and ultimately *The Most Ancient Dream*.
